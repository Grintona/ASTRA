CURIOSITY TAKES US FURTHER

ASTRA is a collaborative front-end university project designed and engineered by three students. We envision what civilian spaceflight planning and commercial interplanetary travel will look like in 2030.

Our Story & Vision

ASTRA began with a simple question: what if planning an expedition to the Moon or Mars felt as intuitive, transparent, and aesthetically refined as booking a premium holiday on Earth? We turned that concept into a responsive, accessible, multi-page web platform where visitors can explore destinations, calculate exact fares in real time, and inspect cutting-edge spacecraft systems.

Our Mission

Make interplanetary exploration accessible to imagine through transparent pricing, rigorous technical diagrams, intuitive configuration forms, and responsive design across all devices.

Our Vision

A future where human curiosity transcends Low Earth Orbit, guided by sustainable closed-loop life support, autonomous navigation, and respectful scientific exploration.
