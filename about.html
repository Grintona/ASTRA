Curiosity takes us further

ASTRA is a fictional space travel agency built by three students. We imagine what planning a journey beyond Earth could look like.

Our story

ASTRA began with a question: what if choosing a trip to the Moon felt as clear as planning a holiday on Earth? We turned that idea into a website where visitors can explore destinations, compare missions and plan a journey.

This is a university web development project. The agency, spacecraft and travel offers are fictional; no real trips are sold.

Our mission

Make the idea of space travel easy to explore, with clear destination information, understandable technology and simple journey planning.

Our vision

A future where curiosity brings people beyond Earth, and every journey starts with preparation, care and respect for space.

How we built ASTRA

  • HTML5: page structure, navigation, forms and tables.
  • CSS3: the dark theme, Flexbox, Grid and responsive media queries.
  • Bootstrap 5.3: responsive columns and form components, customized with our CSS.
  • Git & GitHub: shared source code and version history.
