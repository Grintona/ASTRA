Frequently Asked Questions

Pre-flight protocols, physical requirements, and zero-gravity acclimation.

✦ Interactive jQuery Accordion
What medical clearance is required for civilian spaceflight? +

All passengers undergo a cardiovascular stress screening and vestibular balance test at our Houston or Baikonur training facilities. Good general health is required, but Olympic athleticism is not necessary. Passengers with controlled blood pressure or corrective vision are eligible for Lunar and Orbital tiers.

How long is the pre-flight astronaut training program? +

Orbital Sanctuary and Lunar Horizon require a 5-day comprehensive orientation (centrifuge simulation, parabolic zero-g aircraft sessions, and emergency hatch drills). Interplanetary voyages to Mars require 3 weeks of biomechanical acclimation and closed-loop greenhouse protocols.

What is included in the base fare versus optional add-ons? +

Base fares cover roundtrip rocket launch, custom-fitted biometric spacesuit, private stateroom berth, gourmet freeze-dried & hydroponic dining, and telemetry telemetry support. Add-on packages provide extra EVA spacewalk hours, VIP Earth-relay broadband, and comprehensive cosmic health insurance.

Can I stay connected with family on Earth during the flight? +

Yes. In Low Earth Orbit and around the Moon, high-throughput laser communications provide low-latency video calls and internet access. For Mars and Europa, signal transmission takes between 4 and 45 minutes each way due to light speed; voice messages and media packets are delivered in batch streams.

Your Journey Milestones

Phase 01

Pre-Flight Training & Suit Fitting

Five days of intensive zero-gravity simulator training and pressurized spacesuit tailoring at our spaceport.

Phase 02

Orbital Insertion & Stage Separation

Launch atop heavy-lift reusable booster. Within 9 minutes, experience pure sustained microgravity in Earth orbit.

Phase 03

Trans-Lunar / Mars Injection Burn

Ignition of the high-efficiency ion drives towards your destination, accompanied by orbital champagne service.

Phase 04

Planetary Arrival & Return

Controlled descent to target outposts, surface exploration, and hypersonic atmospheric entry back to Earth.
